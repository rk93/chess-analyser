Privacy Policy

Chess Analyser · Kiranova

Effective date: 21 October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Chess Analyser website and Android application (the “App”) use information when you analyse chess positions, import games, or access third-party chess services. The Android version displays the web app hosted at rk93.github.io/chess-analyser/.

Information you provide

  • Chess.com username and public games. If you enter a Chess.com username and import games, the App requests publicly available game information from the Chess.com public API. Imported games and preferences can be saved in your browser or app storage.
  • PGN and chess positions. Games or positions that you paste or analyse may be processed locally. If an online analysis option is used, chess-position information may be sent to the selected external chess analysis service.
  • Optional Lichess connection. If you choose to connect Lichess, you are redirected to Lichess for authorisation. An access token may be stored locally to enable the connection. You can disconnect from within the App.

Storage and processing

The App uses browser-based local storage, including localStorage and IndexedDB, to remember preferences, game data, cached analyses, and (when you choose to connect Lichess) the authorisation token. This information is stored on your device or in your browser profile. Clearing the App's or browser's site data removes this locally stored information. The App does not require you to create a Kiranova account.

Third-party services

Depending on the features you use, the App connects to services provided by others, including:

  • Chess.com — retrieval of publicly available game data;
  • Lichess and its opening explorer — optional authorisation, opening statistics, and chess data;
  • Third-party chess analysis services (including chess-api.com, when selected or used as fallback) — analysis of chess positions;
  • GitHub Pages and third-party content delivery networks — hosting and delivery of web assets or dependencies.

When your device connects to these providers, they may receive technical information such as your IP address, request metadata, and the chess information needed to fulfill the request. Their handling of information is governed by their own policies. Local Stockfish analysis does not require sending a position to an online chess analysis provider.

Advertising and payments

The App does not currently offer in-app purchases or serve third-party advertising through its own interface.

Sharing

Information is sent to third-party services only as needed to provide the features described above. Kiranova does not sell user information. External services may process requests according to their own privacy policies.

Your choices and deletion

You can use many features without connecting a Lichess account. You may disconnect Lichess within the App and clear local App/site data through your device or browser settings. Clearing local data removes locally stored games, settings, caches, and tokens; it does not delete information already held independently by external services such as Chess.com or Lichess.

Children

The App is intended as a general chess learning and analysis tool. Children should use any external third-party account features in accordance with those providers' terms and with appropriate parental guidance.

Security and changes

Connections to the App and the named third-party services use HTTPS. No networked application can guarantee absolute security. We may update this policy as features change, and the latest version will appear on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests concerning Chess Analyser, contact the developer via the Chess Analyser issue tracker. Do not post passwords, account tokens, or other sensitive information in public issues.
